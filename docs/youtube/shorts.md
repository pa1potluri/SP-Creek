# SP Creek Shorts: 10 ideas

Short, calm moments (15–30 seconds) cut from the episode presets in `world/src/presets.js`.
Shot numbers are the numbers shown in the Camera panel (the preset files store them one lower,
so panel shot 66 is `shot:65`). Episode times point at where that shot starts in the episode
render, so a Short can be cut straight from the finished episode file.

**How to make one:** render the episode (or play the single shot from the Camera panel and
record it), then crop to 9:16 in editing, keeping the main subject in the middle third. The
renderer has no vertical mode yet, so pick moments where the subject sits near the centre.
Each Short should link back to its full episode as the related video.

## Description for every Short

```text
A tiny calm moment from SP Creek, a cosy painted valley for kids. No talking, nothing scary, just gentle sounds. Watch the full episode on our channel! #SPCreek #KidsVideos #Shorts
```

## Settings for every Short

- **Made for Kids:** Yes
- **Altered or synthetic content:** Yes (the whole world is computer-made 3D animation)
- Keep the same tags as the full episode, plus `shorts`.
- No loud music or sudden sound at the start: Shorts autoplay, so keep the first second as gentle as the rest.
