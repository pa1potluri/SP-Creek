# 🤝 Join SP Creek

SP Creek starts small: one family, a painted valley and a children's channel. We want it to grow into a big,
gentle world that children everywhere can watch and play in, safely. **If you like what you see, come and grow it
with us.**

## Ways to join

| | Who | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 💡 **Friend** (free) | Everyone | ⭐ star, react to the weekly updates, vote and share ideas |
| 🎨 **Creator** (free) | Artists, musicians, storytellers, teachers | Paint a texture, draw a character, compose a calm tune, write a story for a video. You are credited by name. |
| 🛠️ **Builder** (free) | Developers | Fix a bug or build a small feature from the Ideas list (we review everything) |
| 🌱 **Supporter** | Anyone | A small monthly amount through **GitHub Sponsors** (tiers below) |
| 🏛️ **Partner** | Schools, studios, kids' brands, educators | Work with us on content, languages or a learning version. Write to us in Discussions → Partners. |

## Supporter tiers (GitHub Sponsors)

| Tier | Per month | Thank you |
|---|---|---|
| 🌼 Seedling | $2 | Your name on the "Thank you" board in the README |
| 🦆 Duck pond | $5 | + Early look at each weekly update |
| 🎈 Balloon | $15 | + Vote twice in the monthly "what to build" poll |
| 🏡 Founding family | $50 | + Your name on a little sign in the town (after approval, see below) |

**About names on town signs:** a Founding family name goes on a sign **only after the project owner has
approved it**. Names must be child-friendly, with no brands, links, slogans or anything unkind. We may shorten a
name or politely say no, and then the other thank-yous of the tier still apply.

**Where the money goes:** AI tool subscriptions (Claude, ChatGPT), painted art, music, and later hosting for the
online play version. We post a short summary every few months.

> **Please note:** support is a gift to help the project, not an investment. It does not buy a share of the
> project or its income. Partner agreements are made one by one, in writing.

## Our promise to children and families

No weapons, no fighting, nothing scary, no ads inside the world, no data collected from children.
