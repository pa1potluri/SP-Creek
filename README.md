# 🌄 SP Creek

![SP Creek main street at golden hour](docs/images/town-day.png)

**Explore, build and unwind in a gentle, hand-painted 3D valley for children.**

## [▶ Play now](https://pa1potluri.github.io/SP-Creek/)

Free to play in your browser. Nothing to install; best on a computer or tablet.

## A little valley to make your own

- 🗺️ Explore a cosy town, firefly forest, waterfall lake, sunny cove and balloon hill.
- 🔨 Build your own corner of town with houses, plants, paths and friendly props.
- 🎨 Choose oil painting, Dreamy, Anime, Blocky, Paper Cut or Pixel Art.
- 🌅 Enjoy day and night, seasons, gentle weather and calm ready-made videos.
- ⭐ Find hidden stars, meet animal friends and decorate your cottage.
- 🧸 A calm, child-friendly world with soft colours and friendly characters.

Layout v2 keeps the world in view with picture-led navigation and nearby controls.

## Run locally

```bash
python serve_world.py 8810
```

Open [localhost:8810](http://localhost:8810). The game uses local libraries and assets, with no build step.

## How it is made

SP Creek is a **pilot project by one family**, made with AI helpers:
**Claude** (lead and reviewer), **ChatGPT Codex** (builder) and **ChatGPT** (painted textures). Built with
[Three.js](https://threejs.org). Every change is reviewed and tested before it goes in.
