Backdrop Stitcher

Joins your ChatGPT backdrop tiles into one seamless 360° painting for SP Creek. It finds where each tile continues the last, cuts along the least-visible line (no doubled objects, no cross-fade ghosts), evens out the colour, and makes the last tile run straight into the first.

How the tiles joined

Overlap = how many pixels of the next tile repeat the previous one. Shift = how far the next tile sits lower (+) or higher (−). If a join looks wrong, type your own numbers and press Re-stitch.
