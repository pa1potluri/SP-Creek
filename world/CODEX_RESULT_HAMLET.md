# Willow Creek hamlet

Added a default seven-building hamlet west of the north road, centered at (-116, 65): a chapel, four differently coloured houses, a barn, and WILLOW CREEK STORE. A curved dirt lane joins the existing north road. Two benches, two lane lanterns, and six dedicated walkers make it a separate small community.

The clearing is softly flattened and cleared of overlapping vegetation. Its footprint avoids the town, river channel, railway, ranch, and camp. The Town-life checkbox “Willow Creek hamlet” defaults on and is saved with life settings. Hiding it removes its buildings, road, benches, walkers, colliders, floors, emitters, and terrain pads; showing it restores them without duplicating physics.

Hamlet scenery stays outside the user layout arrays and cannot be selected/deleted as a user build. Project reloads preserve it. Building specifications live in `src/hamlet.js` and use the existing Build-mode builders, leaving one building implementation to extend for the upcoming catalogue task.

Appended shots 22–24 (indices 21–23), after Meet the Cast, in the Willow Creek group: Approach, Chapel orbit, and Overlook. Existing shot numbers and video presets remain unchanged. The appended Village at dusk preset uses Oil, dusk, gentle wind, place ambience, and three 90-second clips (4½ minutes); its first clip cuts in. The optional valley flight was omitted to keep the video focused on the hamlet.

No showroom or ambience code was changed. Existing pan shots retain their previous framing; the new overlook uses an optional downward look offset.
