# Credits

SP Creek is made by the Potluri family with AI helpers: Claude (lead and reviewer), ChatGPT Codex (builder)
and ChatGPT (painted textures and plants, from our own prompts).

SP Creek loads these open-source libraries from the jsDelivr CDN. They are not changed and not copied into
this repository.

| Library | Version | Used for | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) | 0.160.0 | the 3D world | MIT |
| [mp4-muxer](https://github.com/Vanilagy/mp4-muxer) | 5.1.3 | saving videos as MP4 | MIT |

Sounds are made in code (Web Audio); sign lettering uses fonts already on the viewer's computer. No other
third-party pictures, sounds, fonts or code are included.
