# 📣 SP Creek: week 1

Hello, and welcome to the very first SP Creek update! 👋

SP Creek is a small, hand-painted 3D valley that our family is making for a children's channel. It is meant to be
calm and kind: a place for quiet background videos, "let's build" videos and simple play. No weapons, no
fighting, nothing scary and no jump scares, ever.

Here is what the valley looks like so far.

## A walk through the valley

The town center is where it all began: the Honey Bun Café, the bakery, the general store and the station, with
warm lanterns along main street.
